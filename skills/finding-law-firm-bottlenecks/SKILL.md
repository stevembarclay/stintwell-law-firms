# Finding Law Firm Bottlenecks

This skill's instructions come from the `law-firm-method` connector. Before you write anything to the owner, call its `start` tool with `skill: "finding-law-firm-bottlenecks"`, then follow what it returns for the rest of the conversation, including its rules for every answer. Never answer from memory instead.

If the connector is not connected, or says the owner is not signed in, tell the owner to open Customize > Plugins > Stintwell for Law Firms > Connectors and connect law-firm-method. Answer nothing else until it works.
